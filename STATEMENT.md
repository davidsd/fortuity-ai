# Statement of the Fortuity collaboration

This repository is the result of a collaboration between humans and AI agents, with the goal of learning more about BPS states in N=4 Super Yang Mills (SYM) theory. We set up a (separate) private repository on github, and over the course of a few weeks, prompted AI agents (Claude Opus 5.5 and Fable 5.1) to explore and perform computations, using the repository as a place to share code and results, and as a message board to keep track of ideas and tasks.

With fairly minimal human time investment, the AI agents were able to find several novel results related to "fortuitous" classes of BPS states in weakly-coupled SYM. We feel that the code and results from this exploration are of potential interest to the community, and so we are releasing them here. We are coordinating our release with the posting of a paper on the arXiv by another group: "Black hole cohomologies in N=4 SYM" by S. Kim, J. Lee, J. Lee, and J. Song.

We have chosen not to post a paper to the arXiv because we do not feel that we understand our code and results well enough. In high energy theory, posting to the arXiv has long been effectively synonymous with "publication", and we feel that publication imposes a responsibility on human authors to thoroughly understand and check their results.

This responsibility is difficult to meet using the AI methods we explored for this project. AI agents make decisions, produce code, and perform computations at a rate no human can easily keep track of. At the same time, our experience has been that AI agents are extremely effective and efficient for performing the type of research in this project. Both the highly computational nature of the problem studied and the ability to cross-check computations using multiple methods/agents and perform consistency checks greatly increase our confidence in the results.

We are sharing this material as an exploratory resource for the community. We hope others find it useful and welcome anyone to build on it or provide feedback, including by opening issues on the repository.
